About Gujarat

Gujarat is a beautiful state in western India. It is famous for its rich history, traditional culture, festivals, delicious food and beautiful tourist destinations.

Famous Tourist Places

Rann of Kutch

A beautiful white desert famous for the Rann Utsav.

Statue of Unity

The world's tallest statue, dedicated to Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel.

Somnath Temple

A famous temple located near the Arabian Sea.

Famous Food

  • Dhokla
  • Fafda and Jalebi
  • Thepla
  • Undhiyu
  • Khandvi

Culture and Festivals

Gujarat is known for Garba, Dandiya Raas, Navratri celebrations, traditional clothing and colourful handicrafts.

Did You Know?

Gujarat is the birthplace of Mahatma Gandhi.
